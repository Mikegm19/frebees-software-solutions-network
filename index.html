🇳🇬 NIGERIA TAX ACT GUIDE

Small Company Tax Benefits

If your yearly turnover stays at ₦100 million or below, you can enjoy:

  • No need to charge VAT on your sales
  • Exempt from 4% Development Levy
  • Relief from certain Withholding Tax (WHT) deductions
  • 0% Company Income Tax (CIT)

⚠️ Important:

These benefits only apply if you file your tax returns. Filing is how you prove you qualify as a small company.
